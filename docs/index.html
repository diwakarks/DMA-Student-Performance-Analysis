Methodological Qualifications, Provenance & Limitations

Academic disclosure regarding data architecture, boundaries, and observational validity

Dataset Architecture & Provenance

The dataset consists of N = 121 student observations across 18 original variables, cleaned and engineered into 27 standardized analytical features. It was compiled for academic instructional evaluation within the Data Mining & Analytics (DMA) Lab curriculum at Dr. B. R. Ambedkar National Institute of Technology (NIT) Jalandhar.

To ensure rigorous ethical integrity, the dataset is qualified as a structured educational scenario designed to test data mining workflows, non-parametric correlation mechanics, multi-variable linear modeling, and unsupervised k-means clustering.

Methodological Boundaries

  • Cross-Sectional Observational Design: Precludes causal assertions. Observed relationships (e.g., screen time and GPA depression) reflect statistical associations rather than proven causal mechanisms.
  • Binned Midpoint Approximations: Variables such as SGPA (e.g., 7.5 for "7-8") and Study Hours (e.g., 2.5 for "2-3 hours") utilize interval midpoint substitution, constraining fine-grained variance.
  • Self-Reporting Vulnerability: Survey-based metrics (daily screen time, sleep duration, regularity) are inherently susceptible to social desirability and recall distortion.
  • Unobserved Confounders: Crucial academic determinants such as socioeconomic background, mental health distress, and course difficulty indices were not recorded.
Institutional Ethics Statement: No personally identifiable student information (PII) is included in this repository. All student records are fully anonymized with synthetic identifiers (1–121) complying with educational data confidentiality standards.
